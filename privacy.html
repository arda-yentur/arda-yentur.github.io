Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Arda Yentur's Game Company ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and shares information when you play our mobile games (the "Games"), and when you visit this website. If you have any questions, contact us at ardayentur@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or other contact details to play our Games, and you do not need an account.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • Our Games show ads and use third-party services for advertising, ad measurement, analytics and attribution. These services collect device and usage information as described below.
  • On iOS, we only access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this at any time in your iPhone Settings.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We never see or store your payment card details.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information collected automatically

When you play, our Games and the third-party services built into them may automatically collect:

  • Device identifiers: the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA, only with your permission on iOS), the Identifier for Vendors (IDFV) and similar identifiers created by our service providers.
  • Device and technical information: device model, operating system and version, language, time zone, screen size, network type, mobile carrier, app version and IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location, such as country or city; we do not collect precise GPS location).
  • Gameplay and usage data: levels played, wins and losses, session length, features and power-ups used, and game events.
  • Advertising data: which ads were shown, whether you watched or clicked them, and related performance information.
  • Attribution data: how you found and installed the Game (for example, which ad campaign led to the install).
  • Purchase data: which in-app items you bought, the price and currency, and the transaction receipt from Apple. We do not receive your name or payment details.
  • Diagnostic data: crash logs and performance information.

2.2 Information you give us

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you write to us. We use it only to answer you.

3. How we use information

  • To run the Games, save your progress and deliver items you purchase.
  • To show ads, including rewarded ads you choose to watch, and to limit how often you see the same ad.
  • With your permission (where required), to show personalized ads based on your interests.
  • To measure the performance of our ads and marketing campaigns (attribution).
  • To understand how players use the Games, fix bugs, balance difficulty and improve the Games.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, including invalid purchases and ad fraud.
  • To answer your support requests and to comply with legal obligations.

5. Advertising and your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): we ask your permission before accessing the IDFA for tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads will still appear, but they will be less personalized. You can change your choice any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Personalized ads: you can also limit ad personalization in Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): where required by law, we ask for your consent before data is used for personalized advertising, using a consent message shown in the Game. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time from the privacy option in the Game's settings, or by contacting us.
  • US state privacy laws: sharing data for targeted advertising may be considered a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information under some US state laws (such as California). You can opt out by declining tracking in the ATT prompt, by changing the setting described above, or by contacting us.

6. Legal bases (EEA and UK players)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we process information on these bases: to perform our agreement with you (providing the Game and purchases); our legitimate interests (keeping the Game working and secure, improving it, measuring campaigns where consent is not required); your consent (personalized advertising and tracking where required); and legal obligations (such as tax records for purchases).

7. Sharing of information

We do not sell your name or contact details. We share information only with the service providers listed above, as needed to run the Games and show and measure ads; when required by law or to protect our rights and players' safety; or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

8. Data retention

Game progress stays on your device until you delete the Game. We and our service providers keep other information only as long as needed for the purposes described above, after which it is deleted or anonymized. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to help you.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email us at ardayentur@gmail.com. Because we do not use accounts, please include your device's IDFV or advertising ID if you can, so we and our partners can locate data linked to your device. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

10. Children

Our Games are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, these transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical information such as your IP address for security purposes (see GitHub's privacy statement). The site loads fonts from Google Fonts.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the effective date above. Significant changes will also be shown in the Game where appropriate.
